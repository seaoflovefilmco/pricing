Questions, answered.

What's included in a wedding videography package?

Sea of Love wedding videography packages include wedding day coverage and your main edited film as the core deliverables, with some collections adding a social reel or other extras. Common add-ons include additional coverage hours, ceremony edits, speech edits, raw footage, and destination travel. The right combination depends on the day you're planning.

What is the deposit and payment structure?

Sea of Love requires a 35% deposit to secure your wedding date, with the remaining balance paid 14 days before the wedding. All payment details and due dates are clearly outlined in writing before booking, so you know exactly what to expect. Payment plans can be discussed if you'd prefer to split the balance into stages.

How long does it take to receive our wedding film?

Your wedding short (1 minute) is delivered within 3 weeks of your wedding. The full wedding film (4–6 minutes) follows within 6 to 8 weeks. We take editing seriously because your film should feel considered and personal, not rushed.

Can we use commercial music in our wedding film?

Sea of Love uses fully licensed music in your main wedding film so the final film can be shared safely on YouTube and Vimeo. For social previews and reels, you can add a commercial song through Instagram's own music library when you post, pairing the preview with a song that feels personal or current. We deliver the social preview in a format that's easy to upload, keeping the main film properly licensed for online use.

Is raw footage available as an add-on?

Yes. Raw footage is delivered as a rendered sequence of selected raw moments rather than every individual camera file. It's designed to be watched back like a home movie, capturing extra candid moments, ceremony sections, speeches, and in-between footage that may not appear in the final edited film. Raw footage is supplied as an unedited product without colour grading, audio mixing, creative editing, or revisions.

How do you handle file storage and backups?

We recommend downloading your wedding films as soon as they're delivered and backing them up in multiple places, since long-term storage isn't guaranteed unless agreed in advance. We do our best to archive project files and footage for a period after delivery. For weddings more than 12 months old, a retrieval fee may apply if final deliverables weren't confirmed or downloaded within the original delivery window.

Can we request changes to our wedding film?

Yes. Every package includes one complimentary round of revisions on the main feature, so you can request small changes after viewing your first cut. Most changes are minor: swapping a shot, adjusting a moment, or refining a section of the edit. Larger structural changes can be discussed and quoted separately if needed.
